01 / About

Engineering first,
architecture when it counts.

I'm a Data Engineer at SunnyData, based in Buenos Aires, with 4+ years building cloud data platforms for enterprise clients, mostly in oil & gas and energy.

Right now I split my time between two things: an agentic customer-support workflow built natively on Databricks, and the architecture and development of a Synapse-to-Databricks migration covering 400 tables and 100 views of Dynamics 365 F&O data.

Before that I led an automated documentation platform at Raízen, tying ADF, Databricks and AI-generated docs into the DevOps Wiki. I also do pre-sales architecture: turning a messy data problem into something both engineers and the business can sign off on.
